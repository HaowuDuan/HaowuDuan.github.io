LLM Notes · Chapter 1

Introduction

Notes on large language models from a physicist's perspective.

This set of notes is a work in progress. It reflects my latest understanding of large language models and records facts and ideas that I want to revisit whenever I need to refresh my memory. I am writing it primarily for myself, but once the full set is complete, I intend to polish it and make it accessible to a broader audience.

Comments, corrections, and suggestions are welcome. You can reach me at hwdcgc@gmail.com.

1. Modern LLM Architecture

A useful technical note should make its assumptions visible before compressing them into notation. Equations belong in the same reading rhythm as prose rather than inside a visually separate application panel.

For a sequence of tokens x1,…,xTx_1,\ldots,x_T, an autoregressive model factors the joint distribution as

p(x1,…,xT)=∏t=1Tp(xt∣x<t).p(x_1,\ldots,x_T)=\prod_{t=1}^{T}p(x_t\mid x_{<t}).
