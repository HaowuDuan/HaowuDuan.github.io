LLM Notes · Chapter 3

Faster Training

A systems-to-algorithms view of reducing the wall-clock time needed to reach a training target.

Useful starting points are Keller Jordan and contributors’ modded-nanogpt NanoGPT speedrun and Andrej Karpathy’s “Let’s reproduce GPT-2 (124M)”.

Pipeline and Systems Efficiency

Data loading, preprocessing, shuffling; host-to-device transfer and prefetching; checkpointing, evaluation, startup/compile overhead; distributed-training and hardware logistics.

Per-Step Compute Efficiency

Custom/fused kernels; FlashAttention; torch.compile and CUDA graphs; mixed precision and memory-layout optimizations; optimizer/kernel fusion.

Training and Model Efficiency

Hyperparameter schedules; optimizer choices; batch size and sequence-length strategy; architectural changes; anything reducing the number of steps needed to reach the target.
